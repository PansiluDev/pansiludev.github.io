“ What is Carbon
Footprint? ”

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, like carbon dioxide and methane, released into the atmosphere by a person, organization, event, or product. It is a measure of the impact human activities have on climate change and is usually expressed in tons of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO₂e).

These emissions come from both direct actions, such as driving a car, and indirect ones, like the energy used to produce the goods and services we consume.

“ How to Reduce
Carbon Footprint? ”

To reduce your carbon footprint, you can make impactful changes by focusing on transportation (walking, biking, or carpooling instead of driving), reducing energy consumption at home (efficiency upgrades and renewable energy), and altering your diet by eating less meat.

Additionally, minimize waste by recycling, reusing, and composting, and make conscious consumer choices by avoiding single-use plastics and buying less.
